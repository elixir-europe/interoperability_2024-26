[ELIXIR](https://elixir-europe.org) is a distributed European infrastructure that helps life scientists manage and use data, software, standards and expertise. This website brings together outputs developed through the [ELIXIR Interoperability Platform’s 2024–26 activities](https://elixir-europe.org/internal-projects/commissioned-services/2024-interoperability), an ELIXIR-funded activity within the [Scientific Programme 2024–28](https://elixir-europe.org/how-we-work/scientific-programme). The work supports people and machines in discovering, accessing, integrating and analysing biological data.

The content reflects expertise from across ELIXIR and lessons from practical FAIRification and data-reuse use cases. It is intended to help researchers, bioinformaticians and data stewards translate the FAIR principles—making research outputs Findable, Accessible, Interoperable and Reusable—into practical decisions and actions.

Explore the [Portfolio]({% link pages/portfolio/portfolio.md %}) to find FAIR-enabling resources and guidance; use the [Framework]({% link pages/framework/framework.md %}) to plan and carry out FAIRification activities; and browse the [Stories]({% link pages/stories/stories.md %}) for examples, experiences and lessons from practice. Together, these sections connect useful resources, a structured process and real-world applications of FAIR data management.
